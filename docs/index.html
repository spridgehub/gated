Outlook

How it's worked out: 7 and 30-day moves come from a repeat-car price index, which follows the same cars day to day, so one cheap arrival or a dear car selling doesn't count as a market move. "Next 30 days" carries that index's recent trend forward, with the range showing how noisy it has been. It reads the trend in asking prices and is not a valuation. Cut risk on each car flags asks well above the model median, long listings, views behind its peers and earlier cuts.
